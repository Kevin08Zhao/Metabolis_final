# D-06 PixelLab Concept-Scene Prompt

## English Prompt

Create one cohesive local environment scene for “Metabolis,” a warm, lively 16-bit- and 32-bit-inspired pixel-art city-building game that represents body mechanisms as friendly urban infrastructure rather than anatomy: show one heart interpreted as a compact civic pump station with paired pumping chambers and visible mechanical rhythm cues, two one-tile-wide arterial transport highways branching outward from the pump station in clearly readable directions, and one separate organ construction zone in the blueprint state, shown as an empty grid-aligned footprint with a closed perimeter, unfinished hatch pattern, corner markers, and oxygen-blue planning lines, with no finished organ occupying it. Use an orthographic three-quarter top-down view with no perspective distortion; align every structure edge, road centerline, junction, footprint, and outline to one consistent square tile grid, make every width and height an integer multiple of that tile unit, place the pump station on a 3-by-3-tile footprint, and keep the entire scene readable at native pixel resolution. Render with crisp hand-placed pixel clusters, limited stepped shading, hard integer-pixel edges, and a single uniform dark outline color #140F1D around all exterior silhouettes; do not use colored per-object outlines. Use exactly and only this locked 22-color palette: #340106, #BA3A3F, #C25453, #48A5CF, #7AD1FD, #CDD9E1, #29314A, #404586, #53548C, #91465F, #BE6E87, #C98197, #B26C09, #E2953A, #DDAD7E, #73CD9B, #B1FFD1, #F4FFF8, #140F1D, #514854, #817582, #E8DCCF; use arterial coral for the pump station and outgoing highways, tissue pink for organic terrain and walls, oxygen blue for the blueprint-state construction markings, and neutrals for non-semantic structure and depth. Keep the mood warm, organic, non-gory, and unmistakably urban, with silhouettes readable without labels. Forbidden elements: realistic anatomical detail, anatomical cutaways, medical illustration, blood, wounds, exposed tissue, bones, people, characters, human figures, faces, text, labels, numbers, interface elements, HUD panels, gradients, anti-aliasing, feathered edges, blur, motion blur, depth-of-field blur, photorealistic lighting, smooth vector curves, glow colors, any color outside the locked palette, sprite sheets, alternate views, or additional asset studies.

## Rationale

The prompt establishes the “body mechanism as urban infrastructure” metaphor first. A pump-station silhouette, paired chambers, and mechanical rhythm cues express the heart without pushing the model toward realistic anatomy. The two roads are one tile wide, branch outward from the station, and expose readable direction. A closed boundary, unfinished texture, corner markers, and blue planning lines identify the blueprint-state construction zone without relying on text or interface elements.

The camera is a distortion-free orthographic three-quarter top-down view. Every edge, road centerline, and footprint must align to one square grid. The prompt includes all 22 locked hex values and assigns responsibilities to the heart, roads, tissue terrain, blueprint lines, and neutrals, reducing the chance that PixelLab invents colors or exchanges semantic roles. The forbidden list covers the main failure modes: medicalization, gore, people, text and UI, and smooth rendering.

## Three-Minute Decision Checklist

- [ ] **Subject and composition:** Without reading text, identify one pump-station heart, two transport highways branching outward from it, and one empty blueprint-state construction zone with a closed boundary and unfinished texture. The image contains no realistic organ details, blood, people, or faces.
- [ ] **Pixels and grid:** At 100% and 400% zoom, every exterior outline is `#140F1D`; road width and subject footprints align to one square grid; no edge has anti-aliasing, blur, a gradient, or a subpixel offset.
- [ ] **Palette:** Sample one pixel each from the heart body, road, construction blueprint line, tissue terrain, and global outline. Every sample exactly matches one of the 22 locked hex values; one mismatch fails the result.

## Three Tuning Directions

1. If the result resembles an organ or medical illustration, strengthen “friendly urban infrastructure rather than anatomy” in the first sentence and replace “civic pump station” with “clearly mechanical municipal pump station with building walls, pipes, and road junctions.”
2. If the result is too smooth or illustrative, strengthen “Render with crisp hand-placed pixel clusters…” by adding “use one-pixel stair-step diagonals and no intermediate edge pixels.”
3. If the result contains out-of-palette colors or swaps semantic colors, strengthen “Use exactly and only this locked 22-color palette…” by adding “reject and regenerate any pixel whose hex is not listed, with no automatic color blending.”
